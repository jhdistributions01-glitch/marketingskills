# TikTok Ads Benchmarks & Feature Data — 2026

Data collected June 2026. Verify before using in budget decisions.

## Cost benchmarks (medians)

- CPM: ~$9.16 (good range $3–15) — notably cheaper than Meta (~$14+)
- CPC in-feed: ~$1.02 (range $0.20–2.00); Beauty $0.74, Retail $0.79, Finance $1.71, Legal $1.92
- CPCs run ~40–50% below Meta
- CTR: ~0.61% average; 0.5–1.5% typical; >2% excellent
- CVR: 0.5–5% (e-comm/fashion high end)
- Search Ads: CPC $0.90–2.50, CVR 2–6%

## Spark Ads

- ~30% higher completion rates, ~142% higher engagement vs non-Spark
- ~2.4x CTR, ~1.4x CVR, ~44% higher conversion rates vs standard in-feed
- No duration restrictions (inherits organic post)

## GMV Max (TikTok Shop)

- Automates Shop promotion around total GMV/ROI: orchestrates paid ads, organic videos, affiliate posts, LIVE, coupons
- ~30% higher GMV vs manual Shop campaigns; up to ~20% incremental GMV
- Typical corridor: 3–5x ROI, $8–25 CPO
- 2026 additions: Creative Hub, Spillover Reporting, upgraded LIVE; pro features in testing factor seller costs (affiliate, coupons, fees)

## Smart+ / 2026 product announcements

- Smart+: automated performance suite (targeting, bidding, creative rotation) — Advantage+/PMax equivalent
- TikTok World 2026: TopReach, Smart+ upgrades, Symphony (AI creative), GMV Max expansion, Search Hubs

## Specs

- 9:16, 1080×1920, MP4/MOV, H.264/H.265, ≥2,000 kbps, ≤500MB
- In-Feed duration: up to 10 minutes (old 5–60s ceiling removed); optimal 15–34s
- Safe zones: top ~130px, bottom ~440px, right ~44px
- External watermarks = automatic rejection (non-Spark)
