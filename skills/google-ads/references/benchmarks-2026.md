# Google Ads Benchmarks & Feature Data — 2026

Data collected June 2026. Verify before using in budget decisions.

## Search benchmarks (cross-industry)

- Average CPC: $2.96–$4.22 depending on source/quarter — +12% YoY, steepest rise since 2021
- Drivers: AI Overviews reducing organic CTR (pushing budget to paid), new AI-era advertiser demand, PMax competition for inventory
- Highest CPC verticals: Legal ($6.75), Consumer Services ($6.40)
- Lowest: E-commerce ($1.16)
- YoY movers: Real Estate +27%, Education −23%, Beauty & Personal Care −19%, Automotive-for-sale −6%

## AI Max for Search

- Feature suite inside Search campaigns (not a campaign type); features toggleable individually
- Search term matching (keywordless tech) + asset optimization (text customization, final URL expansion)
- Keywords retain priority; AI Max fills incremental queries only
- Reported: ~14% more conversions at similar CPA/ROAS; ~27% for exact/phrase-heavy campaigns
- Dynamic Search Ads auto-upgrade to AI Max from September 2026
- Available globally, no spend minimums

## Smart Bidding Exploration

- Expanded 2026: PMax (open beta), PMax with feeds + Shopping (betas rolling out)
- Search campaigns using it: ~27% more unique converting users

## Brand safety

- Text guidelines (announced Sept 2025) expanding to all advertisers through Q1 2026, including PMax
