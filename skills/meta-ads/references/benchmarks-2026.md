# Meta Ads Benchmarks & Feature Data — 2026

Data collected June 2026. Verify before using in budget decisions.

## Cost benchmarks (cross-industry medians)

- CPM: ~$14.19 (+20% YoY from $11.82)
- CPM sales campaigns: $20–30; strong creative (CVR 10–15%) holds ~$25, weak creative spikes >$50
- CPC (Facebook): ~$1.72 (from $1.55 in 2025); Instagram higher: $1.83–$3.35
- CPA median: ~$38.19 (+8.5% YoY)
- ROAS sales median: ~2.79
- CVR range: Fitness 14.29%, Education 13.58% … Hardware/Automotive 0.37%
- CPC floor: Apparel $0.45, Food & Beverage $0.52; ceiling: Finance/Insurance $3.77
- CPA efficient verticals: Lifestyle/Boutique ~$30, Baby ~$30; expensive: Electronics ~$49, Travel Accessories ~$48
- Instagram Stories: ~61% higher CTR than Facebook Feed

## Andromeda (delivery system, fully rolled out ~Oct 2025)

- Creative-based delivery replaced audience-based targeting: system reads creative to predict audience
- Levers that matter: creative diversity (10–20 conceptually distinct actives), simplified structure (ASC primary), signal quality (Pixel + CAPI, EMQ ≥ 7)
- Weak signal slows the learning loop and inflates CPMs

## Advantage+

- ASC: ~17% lower CPA vs manual on average; up to ~32% lower in mature e-comm verticals (2026 data)
- Advantage+ Creative: auto-variants (text, music, ratios, visual) — audit each enhancement

## Creative specs

- 9:16 primary (~90% of inventory vertical; ~98% mobile users); 4:5 for Feed
- Unified 9:16 safe zone (March 2026): avoid top 14%, bottom 20–35%, sides 6%
- Center-square method: critical content within centered 1080×1080 on 1080×1920 canvas
- Sound: Meta reports 75%+ of Instagram Reels views are with sound (the old "~85% watched muted" figure is 2016 feed video). Keep burned-in captions for accessibility and muted viewers; design for sound on
- Refresh 2–3 new creatives weekly at scale; SMB budgets: change when "Fatiga creativa" appears or cost per result rises for several days
